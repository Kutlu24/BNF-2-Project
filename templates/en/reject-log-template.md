# Reject log: template

A reject log records which hits you **discarded** and why, above all the hits that looked plausible. It makes your selection traceable. Keep it as a section of your search log. Literature Research asks for at least 5 entries. Write each entry right after the decision.

## Reasons for rejection (use these labels)

- **wrong period:** the work deals with another time than your question.
- **wrong arena:** the work deals with another country, institution or topic.
- **popular science only:** no references, no method, no way to check the claims.
- **cannot be verified:** you found no trace in a catalogue, on a DOI or on the publisher page.
- **invented reference:** an AI answer gave a reference that does not exist. Note the AI log entry.
- **language or access:** you cannot read or obtain the work in the time available.
